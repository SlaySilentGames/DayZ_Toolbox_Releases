# DayZ Toolbox - Roadmap

What's been added lately, what's being worked on, and what's planned. Plans change: things move up when server
owners ask for them, and some ideas get dropped if the game or Nitrado can't support them. There are no dates; each
version is ready when it's tested.

**Have an idea or found a problem?** In the toolbox, use **Help → Send feedback or report a problem**. No account
needed.

---

## Recently added

### 1.5.2
- **A safety net before restarting outside your restart plan:** with players on, a push or restart says how many
  and when the next planned restart is, and can wait for it (the push then starts a minute before, after players
  have had their warnings).
- **The endgame gauntlet:** choose how many jump-ups each room has; its wolves and bears now appear.
- **Fixed:** item lists too narrow to read; custom vehicles not coming back while players were nearby; the
  military outpost's barriers could be climbed.

### 1.5.1
- **Weather: choose how often it changes**, from every 15 minutes to every 12 hours, or any number of minutes, so
  the weather can come in long blocks. (A player's idea, sent with **Send feedback**.)
- **The endgame gauntlet has a default spot on Badlands**, with every height already checked in game.
- **Fixed:** switching between light and dark took seconds and could freeze the window; after opening the File
  Editor, F1 and keyboard shortcuts could stop working.

### 1.5.0
- **The endgame gauntlet:** a ready-made challenge area to place on your map. Five walled arenas and a dark final
  room, joined by gates and shipping-container hallways, with a key, locked loot vaults, rising numbers of infected,
  wolves and a bear, and toxic gas. Choose each room's enemies and each vault's loot, or leave a vault empty as a
  decoy.
- **Fix placed objects with emotes:** if something floats, sinks or leaves a gap, stand next to it in game and do
  an emote. The toolbox reads your server's logs and corrects the heights there.
- **Starting loadouts that work:** extra items are packed into the character's bags and pockets so they actually
  spawn, and a picture of the character shows what's in each slot.
- **A warning when you close with work left:** unsaved changes, or changes your server doesn't have yet.

### 1.4.0
- Undo your last push to the server.
- Send feedback or report a problem from inside the toolbox.
- Loot & Difficulty presets.
- The POI Importer: bring in POIs people share and place them anywhere on your map.
- Ready-made player bases in four styles and three sizes.
- Better ground heights for placed objects.

### Earlier
- Custom vehicle spawns, saved item sets for loadouts and the Item Remover (1.3.0).
- Player Activity: players, trails, kills, deaths and bases on the map, from your server's logs (1.2.0).
- Changing your server's map from the toolbox (1.1.0).
- The first release: installer, automatic updates, the setup wizard and the main pages (1.0.0).

## Being worked on

- **The endgame gauntlet:** testing it in game, room by room, on a live server. Wolves and bears in their own rooms
  are the newest part.
- **Default spots for the gauntlet** on more maps (Badlands has one since 1.5.1).

---

## Planned

- **Copy your setup to another server:** starting loadouts, loot amounts, removed items and settings, from one of
  your servers to another. Copied as changes, so each map keeps its own items.
- **See everything on every map:** show infected zones, gas zones, POIs, bases and spawn points on any page's map,
  so you can see what's already near the spot you're working on.
- **A health summary on the Home page:** your server's status and map, changes not pushed yet, the latest checks,
  and when you last pushed and pulled.
- **Copy your test server's changes to your live server,** picking what to bring.
- **Bans, whitelist and priority queue** with player names from your logs and notes on why.
- **More from Loot & Difficulty:** one-click styles (Casual, Vanilla, Hardcore, PvE) and sliders by category.
- **Player Activity:** a chat log, a map of where people die, and which bases are actually used.
- **More ready-made POIs and bases:** themed hotspots (a camp with infected and a loot crate), roadblocks, landmarks,
  guaranteed loot spots.
- **Plugins from other people:** a safe way to add pages others have made, with a clear warning about what a plugin
  can see and change.

---

## Ideas being considered

- Restricted areas with teleports.
- A community tab: find shared POIs and files from inside the toolbox.
- PC servers (FTP, mods). The toolbox is console-first; PC support is a bigger step.
- A signed installer, so Windows stops showing "Windows protected your PC".

---

## Not possible on console

Traders, custom weapons or items, new buildings and script changes all need mods, which console servers can't run.
The toolbox sticks to what console servers can really do.
